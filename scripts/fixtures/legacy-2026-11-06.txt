对话主题
周末去公园、野餐、骑自行车、突然下雨
错误与正确表达
我说的
正确表达
I go to park with my friend last Saturday.
I went to the park with my friend last Saturday.
We bring many food.
We brought a lot of food.
the weather is very sun
The weather was very sunny.
We sit on the grass and eat sandwich.
We sat on the grass and ate sandwiches.

I rode a bike around the lake. / I went cycling around the lake.

Suddenly it started to rain.

重点词汇
• picnic：野餐
• park：公园
• lake：湖
• grass：草地
• sandwich：三明治（可数）
• sunny：晴朗的
• umbrella：雨伞
• suddenly：突然
• cycling：骑自行车
• soaked：湿透的
词组与固定搭配
• go for a picnic：去野餐
• ride a bike：骑自行车
• start to rain：开始下雨
• get out of the rain：躲雨
• a lot of：很多
• have a great time：玩得很开心
语法
• 一般过去时：go → went，bring → brought，sit → sat，ride → rode，run → ran
• food 一般不可数：a lot of food，不说 many food
• sandwich 可数：two sandwiches
• 表示目的用 to do：to get out of the rain
完整英文段落
Last Saturday I went to the park with my friend. We went for a picnic and brought a lot of food. The weather was very sunny, so we sat on the grass and ate sandwiches.
After lunch, I rode a bike around the lake. It was really relaxing. Suddenly it started to rain, and we didn't have an umbrella.
We ran to a coffee shop to get out of the rain. We were a little soaked, but we had a great time.
